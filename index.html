About Me

Bridging veterinary clinical science with modern molecular pathology and bioinformatics.

I am a veterinary graduate and Master’s student in Pathology at Sylhet Agricultural University, Bangladesh, driven by a deep commitment to One Health sustainability, translational veterinary research, and combatting global health threats like antimicrobial resistance (AMR).

My research journey spans both wet-lab diagnostics—such as PCR, ELISA, microbiological isolation, and cell culture—and dry-lab bioinformatics, including next-generation sequencing (NGS), metagenomics, phylogenetics, and vaccine design using R, Python, and Linux.

Supported by prestigious competitive grants including the National Science and Technology (NST) Fellowship and the World Bank & Higher Education Acceleration Grant, I aim to pursue doctoral studies (PhD) to advance diagnostics, alternative therapeutics, and infectious disease surveillance.
